Code cheat sheet

game.player = "🐸";
Who runs. An emoji, a picture name, or a color like "#ff4f8b" for a square.
game.speed = 8;
How many squares you move each second.
game.jumpHeight = 2.2;
How many squares high a jump goes.
game.gravity = 1;
Bigger falls faster. 0.5 is floaty, like the moon.
game.sky = "#5ec8ff"; game.sky2 = "#c9f0ff";
Sky colors, top and bottom. Also groundColor, blockColor, spikeColor.
game.trail = "#ffffff";
Color of the trail behind you. Use "none" to hide it.
level(` ... `);
Draw your level with letters. The bottom line is the ground. See "Level letters".
addTile("L", "🔥", "deadly");
Make your own tile. Look can be an emoji, picture name or color. Types: "solid", "deadly", "coin", "bounce", "decoration".
whenJump(function() { ... });
Runs every jump. Also: whenCrash, whenCoin, whenFinish, everySecond.
say("Nice!");
Shows a message on the screen.
playSound("coin");
"jump", "crash", "coin", "pop", "laser", "boing", "kick", "snare", "clap", "hat".
getCoins() getAttempts() getProgress()
Coins this run, tries so far, and how far you got (0 to 100).
randomChance(20) randomNumber(1, 6)
Random surprises: true 20 out of 100 times, or a random number.
game.music = false;
Turn off the soundtrack from the Music tab.

Make a soundtrack

Tap squares to turn sounds on. It loops while you play, restarts with every attempt, and the sky pulses on each kick drum.
